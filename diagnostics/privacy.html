HomeLab Diagnostics privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy describes HomeLab Diagnostics, a personal-use diagnostic tool maintained by jth-67, and these public information pages.

Information accessed and purpose

The tool uses Google account and Drive metadata, including account identifiers, file and folder identifiers, names, revisions, sizes and sharing permissions, to verify the intended accounts and private transport folders. It reads diagnostic request files and writes signed diagnostic result files. Results contain the bounded system information permitted by the owner's selected requests.

The owner's temporary Google authorization uses the Drive read-only scope. The relay account uses the Drive scope, which permits reading, creating, editing and deleting Drive files available to that account. These are broad account-level permissions. The configured folders and the tool's internal checks constrain its intended operations; they do not narrow Google's OAuth grant.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to verify the transport and carry out the owner's diagnostic requests. Request and result files are stored in Google Drive and processed on the owner's systems. The owner may retrieve results through a connected assistant or other chosen client; that service's separate data-handling terms also apply.

The tool does not make diagnostic files public or use Google data for advertising. The public documentation pages do not receive diagnostic requests, results or credentials.

Storage and access control

The owner's systems retain configuration, authentication material, diagnostic history and audit records needed to operate the tool and enforce its checks. Drive retains the request and result files placed there. Access is controlled through the configured account permissions and system protections. Stored copies and backups must be managed where they are held; no automatic deletion period is promised by this policy.

Revoking access and removing data

The owner can remove the relevant authorization through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future access through that authorization but does not automatically delete existing Drive files, local history, logs or backups. The owner can separately stop the tool and manage those stored copies. See Google's connection-management guidance.

Public website

These pages are static documentation hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no sign-in, upload or contact form, analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party content or application-set cookies. GitHub may collect standard request information, including IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy.

Questions and changes

General questions may be raised in the public project repository. Do not include credentials, private file contents or personal information. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.
