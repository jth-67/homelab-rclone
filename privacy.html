HomeLab rclone

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes the personal-use HomeLab rclone integration and this public documentation website.

Purpose and responsibility

HomeLab rclone is maintained by jth-67 for personal HomeLab workflows. It uses rclone to transfer and synchronize selected files between the owner's Google Drive account and the owner's systems.

Google Drive information and use

Google Drive information is used to carry out file-transfer and synchronization operations configured by the owner. Depending on the operations and permissions granted, this can involve file contents and file or folder metadata, such as names, identifiers, sizes, and modification times.

The permissions granted through Google determine what the integration can access. Choosing particular files or folders for a workflow is an operational selection, not a guarantee that the Google authorization grant is limited to those files or folders. Review the Google consent screen and the connection's permissions.

Storage and handling

Workflow files are transferred between the owner's Google Drive storage and the owner's systems. The owner controls the workflow configuration, destinations, and handling of transferred copies. Separate services used in a workflow may have their own data-handling practices and privacy policies.

Copies, operational logs, and backups are managed through the relevant storage systems and their configuration. This policy does not specify an automatic deletion period. Stored copies must be managed or deleted where they are held.

Removing Google access

The owner can review or revoke the integration's access using Google Account third-party connections. Select the relevant connection and remove its access. Google's connection-management guidance explains the available controls.

Revoking the connection prevents future access through that authorization. It does not automatically delete files already transferred to Google Drive or other storage, or copies in logs and backups. Those copies must be removed separately as appropriate.

This documentation website

This site is static documentation. It has no sign-in, upload or contact form, analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party content, or application-set cookies. Visiting it does not authorize access to Google Drive.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub collects standard information about website requests, including visitor IP addresses, for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for GitHub's practices. Google account and Drive services are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Questions and updates

For general questions, use the public project repository. Do not include credentials, private file contents, or personal information in public messages.

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.
